One-page executive summary for detectives

This packet reviews Google Takeout records for April 19, 2024 through April 26, 2024 and compares those Google records with independently recovered phone-message artifacts where timing data was available.

Google's records show Gmail searches and Gmail use during the same week as the recovered screenshot/message sequence. The strongest Google records are Gmail searches for helo payment services ein, Jonathan, Ariana, venmo, and related terms. The phone evidence separately shows screenshot/file transmissions to the conversation labeled Mom, often within seconds of the screenshot filename time.

This packet does not identify the actor. It does not prove whether access was authorized. It does not provide April 2024 IP addresses or device identifiers from Google's records. It does show objective timestamps, source files, and timing correlations that law enforcement can verify against native device/provider records.
